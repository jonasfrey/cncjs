# Easel Material Library - Wood Options

Available wood materials in Inventables Easel:

## Solid & Veneer Woods

- **Bamboo** — Fast-growing grass material, similar to hardwood
- **Birch Plywood** — Light hardwood, clean cuts, good detail
- **Bubinga** — Dense African hardwood, rich color
- **Cherry Plywood** — Medium hardwood, fine grain
- **Hard Maple** — Very dense hardwood, fine detail work
- **Soft Maple** — Lighter hardwood, easier to machine
- **Walnut** — Dark hardwood, premium appearance
- **Pine** — Softwood, easy to cut, less detail capability

## Wood Products

- **MDF** — Medium Density Fiberboard, engineered wood, consistent texture

## Reference: Material Comparison

| Material | Hardness | Detail Quality | Ease of Cut |
|----------|----------|----------------|-------------|
| Pine | Low | Fair | Easiest |
| Bamboo | Medium | Good | Easy |
| Soft Maple | Medium | Good | Easy |
| Birch | Medium-High | Very Good | Medium |
| Cherry | Medium-High | Very Good | Medium |
| Hard Maple | Very High | Excellent | Difficult |
| Walnut | Medium-High | Excellent | Medium |
| Bubinga | Very High | Excellent | Difficult |

## Beech Wood (Buchenholz) Match

Beech is not in the default library. **Use Birch Plywood as the closest substitute** — nearly identical hardness, density, and machining characteristics.
